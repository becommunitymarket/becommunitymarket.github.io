About the Market

Welcome to the Blue Earth Community Market

Every Saturday morning from May through October, the Blue Earth Community Market brings together local growers, bakers, makers, and neighbors at 112 S Grove St. Whether you're picking up fresh vegetables, handmade goods, or just stopping by to say hello, there's always something to discover.

We believe a farmers market is more than a place to shop. It's a place to connect, support local livelihoods, and celebrate what our community grows and creates.

  • Community events and live music
  • Leashed pets welcome
